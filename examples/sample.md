# Grid-frequency forecasting with transformers (draft)

This draft was written with an AI assistant. Some of the references below are real, one is real but
mis-described, one is a real paper that was later retracted, one points at a dead page, and two do not
exist at all. `citetrust examples/sample.md` tells them apart.

The transformer architecture [2] has become the default sequence model. Persistent identifiers for
scholarly objects are discussed in [1].
